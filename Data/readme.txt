1. Water temperature
The file EERES_Temperature.csv includes water temperature measurements in one of the pond mesocosms in EERES. The Water depth at which the measurements were taken (in cm) is provided in the headline. Air refer to air temperature measurements right above the water surface.
The data were measured between 21 July 2020 and 21 June 2021 with 10 min resolution. There are some gaps in the data

2. Meteorological variables
These data were measured by the meteorological station located in EERES (provided both as xlsx and as csv files). Note that values equal to -99999 mark bad/missing data and must be removed.
The data were measured in 2020-2021 with 10 min resolution.

"RE" ist die Aufsummierung des Gesamtregens eines Tages aus der aufgenommenen Menge von "RD". "RD" ist dabei die aufgenommene Regenmenge innerhalb eines Speicherintervalls ( 1 Minute). "RE" wird um 0:00 wieder zurück auf 0 gesetzt.
